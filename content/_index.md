I tinker on database systems and compilers at [RelationalAI](https://www.relational.ai/).

My work spans the "full stack" of a query: from designing the language in which users express their questions, to optimizing them and connecting them to the data layer.

It extends naturally to database internals, observability tools, and scaling software systems more generally.

Before industry, I was a graduate student in programming languages, specifically tackling problems of expressivity:
*"What features does my programming language need to express my problem, and efficiently at that?"* See my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LresJ9cAAAAJ&hl=en).
