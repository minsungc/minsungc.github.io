Hello friends (and friends of friends, or bots who found this site, or anyone/thing else)!

It has been VERY HOT in the city these past couple weeks! Did you guys know that as of 2020, New York City is considered a humid subtropical climate, a distinction it shares with places like Jeju Island, Hong Kong, and Milan? I love that for us! :upside-down-face:

Summer always reminds me of peaches. My mom tells me of her grandparents and their peach farm. She would spend her summers eating different types of peaches in the countryside. There is something so idyllic and carefree about that experience that makes _me_ feel nostalgic. Do you guys ever picture what your parents' childhood was like?

\*takes a second to think about it (I almost teared up not gonna lie)\*

I recently found white peaches (백도복숭아 if you are in the know) at my local gentrificationcore grocery store and I really enjoyed them. They were crunchy, sweet, and the scent of peaches perfumed my apartment for a day or two. As my pollen allergy worsens, so does my allergy to fruits, making the peaches that much more delicious.

Which really makes me want to make jam again! Which makes me want to jam again! Some highlights from the last jam:

- Trying out my homemade strawberry-cherry jam
- Live reading of Ocean Vuong to piano accompaniment
- Impromptu Mitski singalong
- Noodling to find funny patches on my keytar

If any of these appeal to you, please stop by!
