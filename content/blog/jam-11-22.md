Hello friends!

It's November, and things are getting cold...and orange and brown 🎃🍁🍂🦃!

November is a funny transitional between the autumnal whimsy of October and the comfy coziness of December.
The leaves have mostly fallen, the weather doesn't know whether to be hot or cold, people can't decide whether it's too early for Christmas decor or not. It makes me feel...kinda lonely? How does November make you guys feel?

Fun fact though, my birthday on the lunar calendar is November 27, 1999, so I always identified with the year of the rabbit instead of the year of the dragon!

Anyway, how has everyone been? It's been a while since the last jam. Let me know how you've been doing!

I'm starting to get really into tea and am thinking I'll make it the Minsung Hobby™️ of 2027. What do you guys think? I've been really enjoying the fruity and floral ones. There's this jasmine green tea that I've been really enjoying.

I also recently picked up the clarinet again after 8 years of not playing. I sound like doodoo but my fingers still remember all the scales which is really cool! The problem is that now I want to get a bass clarinet, which only adds to my long list of "expensive instruments I want to get but I can't"...

OK I (embarassingly) have been watching the League of Legends World Championship recently too. I refuse to play the game anymore but somehow it's just a sporting event I come back to when it happens, like the World Cup or the Olympics. Am I the only one who does this?

Ugh, I've been rambling too long. Let's jam!
